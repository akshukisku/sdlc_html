Take A Photo That Gets Clear Answers

Do

  • Use natural daylight
  • Fill the frame with the leaf
  • Hold the phone steady
  • Take a few photos if unsure

Don’ts

  • Don’t use the flash
  • Avoid blurry photos
  • Don’t shoot from far away
  • Avoid dark, shadowy light

Smarter Farming , Greater Harvest

Is AgriVision free to use?

Yes, AgriVision offers a free plan with essential features to help you get started. You can explore the platform and use its available tools to support your everyday crop care decisions. As your farming needs grow, you can upgrade at any time to access advanced crop analytics, more detailed reports, and expert recommendations.

Can I check multiple leaves at once?

Right now each photo is checked on its own. For best results, upload one clear leaf per photo and repeat for other leaves.

What crops are supported?

We currently support tomato, potato and pepper, and more crops are being added.

How accurate is the detection?

Results depend on photo quality. Clear, well-lit close-ups give the best predictions, and the result is a guide, not a final diagnosis.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes, the photo is analysed online, so you need an internet connection to get a result.
