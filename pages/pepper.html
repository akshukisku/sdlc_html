Signs to look for

Small, dark or water-soaked leaf spots, often with yellowing and leaf drop.

Healthy class

Healthy

What healthy growth can look like

Evenly colored leaves, steady growth, and no visible spotting or unusual yellowing.

Everyday care

Small habits. Healthier pepper plants.

01

Water at the base

Keep soil evenly moist without waterlogging. Direct water toward the soil rather than the leaves.

02

Give plants room

Allow airflow around your plants. Support climbing or fruit-heavy stems to improve airflow.

03

Check leaves regularly

Look at both sides of leaves for changes. Check lower leaves and remove fallen plant debris.
